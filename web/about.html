About Nirnay

Nirnay means decision. It is a point-of-view demo built for a first conversation with the Retail Lending team of a mid-sized Indian bank about agentic AI in home loan approval.

What it shows

How a bank can cut home loan turnaround and make decisions more consistent and easier to audit, by bringing each file together in a BigQuery lakehouse and letting one credit case agent do the gathering, checking and drafting that takes up most of a credit officer's day. People keep every decision.

Principles

  • The officer decides. The agent recommends, explains and drafts. It never changes a file's status.
  • Every figure has a source. A query, an API response or a document, one click away.
  • Every rule has a clause. Checks cite the bank's policy or the RBI rule they apply.
  • AI reads and writes; code calculates. FOIR, LTV, EMI and routing are deterministic.
  • Policy is data. A rule change reaches files already in progress.

Scope

One product, home loans, from login to the hand-off for sanction, with the applicant's view through to the Key Facts Statement. Documentation, mortgage creation and disbursement are shown as stages but not automated.

What it is not

Not a real bank's system. All customers, documents and numbers are synthetic; the credit policy is illustrative, written for the demo; external services are simulated. RBI rules referenced are real and linked from the policy library.

Who made it

Prepared by a presales business analyst candidate for the Data & AI practice, built with Claude (research, data generation, code, interface and writing) and running on Google Cloud. How it was built.
