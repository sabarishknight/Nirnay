What the applicant sees

The same file from the customer's side: where it is, what was consented to, and the Key Facts Statement once it is sanctioned. No credit jargon, no internal flags.

Why this matters

The most common complaint in home loans is not rejection but silence: applicants do not know where the file is or what is missing. The agent's step log gives the bank a status it can share honestly, stage by stage.

Consent is shown explicitly. Bureau pulls and Account Aggregator fetches happen only against a recorded consent, which is what the Account Aggregator framework and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act expect.

When the officer sanctions, the applicant receives the Key Facts Statement with the all-in Annual Percentage Rate, as RBI requires for every retail loan since 1 October 2024, and has three working days to accept.
