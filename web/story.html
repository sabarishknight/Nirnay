One home loan file, nine systems, eighteen working days.

This is the approach in two acts. First, bring the file together once, in a lakehouse. Then let one agent work the file against your policy, so your credit officer starts from a drafted, sourced note instead of a pile of PDFs. Follow Priya's file on the right as you read.

Act 1

The lakehouse: bring the file together once

Today, the file lives in nine places

Priya applies for ₹68 lakh to buy a flat in Pune. To appraise her, a credit officer opens the loan origination system, core banking, a shared drive of scanned PDFs, the CIBIL portal, the CERSAI portal, and waits on the valuer and the panel advocate. Each source has its own format and its own delay, and the officer re-keys numbers between them. Most of the 18 days is waiting and re-keying, not judgement.

Land everything as it arrives, then refine it in three layers

Every source lands in BigQuery as it is: core banking through change-data-capture, documents as files with the fields read from them, and every bureau or CERSAI response saved exactly as returned. Nothing is overwritten, so any figure in a note for sanction can be traced back to the raw record behind it.

The payoff: one version of the customer, computed one way

Because FOIR, LTV and income are computed in the lakehouse rather than in each officer's spreadsheet, two branches can no longer reach two answers on the same facts. The same layer feeds the lending head's dashboard and, in Act 2, the agent. It also makes the bank's portfolio questions cheap: "which files in progress would breach policy if the repo rate rose?" is one query, not a week of calls.

Act 1 is valuable on its own. A bank can stop here and still get consistency and an audit trail. Act 2 is what turns the data into hours saved.
Act 2

The agent: let it work the file, let people decide

One agent, the same seven steps on every file

We use one well-designed credit case agent, not a swarm. It runs a fixed sequence, because consistency is the point, and fans out only where work is independent: the external checks run in parallel. It reads documents and writes the note with Gemini; every rule and every number is computed in code and cites a policy clause. It never sanctions or declines.

7 Hand offOfficer edits, accepts or overrides with a reason; authority approves deviations; RCU verifies suspected fraud

Where it earns its keep

Checking a clean file faster is useful. The real value is in the files that are not clean, where officers spend most of their time.

Deviations come with a way out

When FOIR is just over the cap, the agent does not stop at "refer". It works out what would bring the file within policy.

Arjun Mehta: FOIR 55.5% vs 55%. Reduce to ₹54.4 lakh, extend to 22 years, or add his wife as co-owner (FOIR 39.9%).

Contradictions surface on day one

Documents are checked against independent sources, not just read.

Sunita Yadav: the seller says the house is unencumbered; CERSAI and the EC show a 2019 mortgage with no release. Legal query raised before sanction, not after disbursement.

Policy changes reach files already in progress

When a rule changes, the agent re-screens every open file in the lakehouse.

RBI raised the repo rate to 5.50% on 7 Oct 2026. Fifteen files in progress now breach their FOIR cap, worth ₹9.5 crore.

People stay in charge, and the agent learns from them

The credit officer sees every check, every source and the drafted note, and can change any of it. Overriding the agent takes one reason, which is stored. Deviations go to the authority in the delegation of powers. Declines need a human and written reasons to the applicant. Suspected fraud goes to the Risk Containment Unit as a check, never as an accusation. Override reasons are reviewed monthly: if officers keep accepting something the policy rejects, the policy or the agent changes.

A pilot measures the real figures.

How we would start: a six-week pilot

Low risk first. The agent runs in shadow mode on one region's home loans before any officer relies on it.

Weeks 1 to 2

Lakehouse slice

Land core banking, loan origination and bureau data for one region in BigQuery (Mumbai region). Encode the home-loan policy as clauses and rules with your credit policy team.

Weeks 3 to 4

Shadow mode

The agent reviews every new file; officers decide as today. We measure agreement, catch-rate on deviations and time per file.

Weeks 5 to 6

Assisted mode

Officers start from the agent's note in two branches. Go or no-go on measured turnaround, override rate and audit findings.
