How this was built

The demo, its data and its documents were produced with Claude working as an agent: searching and reading sources, writing and running code, reviewing its own screens and deploying to Google Cloud. The candidate set direction, made the judgement calls and checked the work. D6 AI usage log (PDF)   D5 research note (PDF)

Where Claude was used

  • Research: RBI circulars, CERSAI, KFS, turnaround benchmarks and the 7 October 2026 repo hike, each verified against a source before it went into the policy pack.
  • Data: a seeded generator for 600 home loan applications with accounts, loans, repayments, bureau, CERSAI, KYC, Account Aggregator, EPFO and GST records, plus PDF documents for four demo files.
  • Lakehouse and agent: SQL for the raw, curated and decision-ready layers, tested locally and run in BigQuery; the credit case agent, rules engine and Policy Watch.
  • Interface and writing: eleven pages, reviewed through screenshots after each pass; this site's copy, the research note and the presenter pack.

What was corrected along the way

  • A citation that credited LTV caps to the wrong RBI circular, caught by reading the circular itself.
  • Guessed RBI links, replaced with verified ones.
  • A first data set with an unrealistic share of files outside policy, rebalanced.
  • A join that produced 606 rows for 600 applications because of colliding IDs, caught by a row-count check.
  • A mock GST service that looked empty because of a field-name clash, spotted on screen.

Gemini inside the product

Inside the demo, Gemini on Vertex AI reads the applicant's PDFs and drafts the note for sanction and answers policy questions. It does not calculate or decide; rules and arithmetic run in code, and people make every decision.
